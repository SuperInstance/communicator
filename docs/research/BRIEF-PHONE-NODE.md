# BRIEF - The phone as a node (Caseys overnight ideation seed, 2026-10-20)

Casey, verbatim sense: he is holding a phone talking to Muse. Could the
phone - not just its mic but BOTH cameras, its speaker, its mics -
become a node? Especially: the rooms fixed cameras can often SEE the
phone in frame. Then the phone is a node of a different kind: its
perspectives mean something different, its mic sits at a specific
location, and the timing of everything has a specific meaning.

Sharpened by Muse for the riff:
- SENSOR: front+rear cameras (first-person room view + his face),
  mic(s), speaker (a sonar emitter/receiver at a moving position).
- BEACON: the only node the fixed cameras can SEE. A known emitter
  that is also visible = the meshs calibration anchor: its chirps
  localize it in the fixed mics; its image localizes it in the fixed
  cameras; the two fixes calibrate each other.
- TIMING AS GEOMETRY: arrival-time differences (phone mic vs room
  mics) turn sound into position; sync events (a clap, a flash - the
  old dialogue protocol generalized) discipline every clock onto the
  one session timeline.
- HONEST CONSTRAINTS to engage, not wave at: simultaneous dual-camera
  capture in a browser varies by phone/OS; clock sync across devices
  is the hard problem AND the trick; the phone moves, so its fixes
  are a track, not a point.
