# ADDENDUM - Awareness, double-hearing, and the calculator doctrine (Casey, 2026-10-21)

## Double-hearing is a sensor
Casey talks to Muse on the phone while the laptop hears the same
voice. The system KNOWS it is hearing one voice twice - and the
DELAY between the arrivals is geometry: range and place from time
difference, the phone-node beacon made concrete. Any source captured
on two devices is a localisation instrument, free.

## Acoustic scene learning
Over time the system learns a spaces sound: kitchen vs desk vs
paper-in-hand are recognisable from the nature of the sound alone.
Learned per space, per person. Place and activity become shading-
layer facts without a camera seeing anything.

## Screen is a mode
Screen capture/reading is a first-class input mode, same class as a
camera view: with screen access the system reads WITH the person, so
they can talk as if it already knows what they are looking at.
Deixis ("this", "here", "put it on the screen") resolves against
whichever modes are live.

## Output follows the person
Awareness routes OUTPUT to the surface matching the located context:
in front of the TV (Jetson attached) -> the big screen; at the
laptop -> pop it up there; phone, headset, computer voice - a modal
projection chosen by where he IS, not by where the agent happens to
live. "Put it on the screen" is only a sane command with location
awareness underneath it.

## THE CALCULATOR DOCTRINE (the capstone)
Awareness grows; processing does not have to. The budget is finite,
and it is NOT for answering questions - it is for BUILDING
CALCULATORS: cheap mechanical detectors and models that need less
energy every generation. Every graduated student becomes a
calculator in the library, fitted per user, per application, per
hardware and API budget. The big models job gets easier as the
library grows; detection streamlines; cost per correct judgment
falls. The teacher-dependence ratio is this doctrines shadow on
the wall. The fleets real product is not answers. It is calculators.
