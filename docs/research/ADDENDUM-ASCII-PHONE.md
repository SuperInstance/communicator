# ADDENDUM - The phone in the ASCII world (Casey, 2026-10-21)

## Phone video becomes an ASCII score stream
The moving phones video converts into the ASCII world - edges and
letters, the Syzygy rendering. ASCII is the SCORE for vision: small,
diffable, readable by teachers and students alike, and cheap enough
for the guided-RL loop to reinforce on at scale. Raw video bankrupts
the loop; the score is what the loop can afford to grade all day.

## Adaptive fidelity (motion-indexed spend)
- Phone STILL: frame rate drops, resolution RISES - stillness buys
  detail. A static scene is described ONCE at high fidelity; the
  live channel then carries only changes (a person crossing the
  frame) - motion-as-text with reference frames, again.
- Phone MOVING: resolution scales back to afford motion - temporal
  resolution over spatial, exactly as the change demands.
The budget follows the change, never the clock.

## Fused pose, honest errors, a walked map
Tilt (lags), GPS (drifts, weak indoors), video (the witness): each
sensor lies differently, so fused they confess. As Casey walks an
unchanging room, his track plus angled ASCII views map the room in
a unique way - the roaming survey, the kayak ground-truth pattern
indoors. The room is the constant; the phone is the surveyor; the
map is the residue. Pose estimates carry their error bars - a fix
is a claim with a confidence, per the shading doctrine.

## Where it runs
Renderer + adaptive policy: edge tier (Jetson weighs it) and phone-
class hardware. Motion-as-text spec gains the adaptive policy.
The evolve page is the natural host for a first ASCII phone probe.
