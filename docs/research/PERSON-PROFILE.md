## 0. THE TWO CLASSES OF DATA

A session somebody performed and a session the page caught are
different kinds of evidence, and they are not averaged together.
The page's own trigger says which is which, so the class is a
reading of the provenance and not a guess.

| class | sessions | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| ambient | 2 | caught first time: the page opened because there was motion or sound, and whatever was said was said unposed. Evidence about the person. |
| drilled | 1 | performed on purpose: the words were chosen and the delivery was on purpose. Evidence about the performance. |

A drilled session is evidence about a performance and an ambient
one is evidence about the person. Both are kept. A single number
averaging them would be evidence about neither.

## 5. WHAT THIS PROFILE DOES NOT SAY

It does not say what the speaker was feeling, and it does not
label the drift with an emotion. A large drift is a measurement,
not a diagnosis. The interpretation is earned slowly: it needs
sessions, and it needs a second opinion, and Casey's own drill
deliveries are the ground truth that would make it mean anything.

It does not average the two classes together either. A session
that somebody performed and a session the page caught are not the
same kind of thing, and a portrait that mixed them would be a
portrait of the average of a person and their performance.
